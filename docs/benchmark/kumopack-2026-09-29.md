# Benchmark — Kumopack vs Packio (reviewed 2026-09-29)

> Source: hands-on walkthrough of kumopack.com, buyer.kumopack.com, mockup.kumopack.com (signed-in buyer account) and the public supplier/pricing/FAQ pages, and the supplier console (`supplier.`) signed in as a **sub-account** of a test factory (settings tabs are restricted to the main account, and the account had no live inquiries, so screens were seen empty).
> **Use**: functional reference only. We do **not** copy their UI, copy text, images, templates, taxonomy wording or code. Every feature we adopt is re-specified in our own words and built to our own design system (`docs/prompts/design-system.md`).
> Verdicts: **Adopt** (we should have it), **Adapt** (same job, different mechanism because our business model differs), **Skip** (does not fit Packio), **Already ahead** (Packio is stronger today).

---

## 1. What Kumopack is

A Thai packaging **marketplace**: buyers post specs, multiple partner factories quote, the buyer picks one, pays the platform, the factory produces and ships, the platform clears money to the factory and takes a transaction fee. Three apps: public site + buyer console (`buyer.`) + supplier console (`supplier.`), plus a 3D mockup studio (`mockup.`, "Kumo's Workshop"). Both sides also pay **subscriptions**.

**Their commercial model (stated publicly on their site):**
- Transaction fee 4 % (OEM, before VAT) / 5 % (marketplace items).
- Clearing every Tuesday 23:30, payout to suppliers on Wednesday; supplier downloads transfer slip + tax invoice in their billing page.
- If the buyer does not confirm receipt within 7 days, receipt is auto-confirmed and clearing proceeds (payout within 7 business days).
- Buyer plans: Free / ฿299 / ฿599 per month — quota of RFQs per day (3 / 10 / unlimited), factories per request (5 / unlimited), seats (1 / 3 / 5), coin multiplier, monthly coupon value, and **dieline / AI-PDF download gated to paid plans**.
- Supplier plans: Free / ฿2,999 / ฿4,999 per month — customer analytics, seeing the buyer's **target price**, number of categories, guarantee badge, banners, personal support.
- Marketplace items ship via a Flash Express API integration, 3–5 business days.
- Samples are sent by the factory before the buyer confirms mass production.
- Disputes: buyer raises a dispute, chats with the factory, escalates to the platform, which decides by its published policy.

**Packio's model is different**: Collective Group (Thailand) is the seller of record, buys at an agreed price from partner factories, runs its own accounting and payouts, and shows the customer one Packio price. So marketplace mechanics (bidding, per-request factory selection, supplier subscriptions, commission) are **not** transferable — but many product mechanics are.

## 3. Where Packio is already clearly ahead
1. **Instant real price** for the launch product types instead of waiting for factory quotes (Kumopack is RFQ-first everywhere in OEM).
2. **Seller of record with full accounting** — ledger, AR/AP, tax, payouts, period close, SAP-free but auditable; Kumopack is a fee-taking intermediary.
3. **Artwork pipeline**: automated preflight, versioned proofs, annotations, approval evidence. Kumopack only takes a ≤ 5 MB artwork file at request time.
4. **Production visibility**: jobs, milestones with photos, QC inspections, scorecards, at-risk alerts.
5. **Logistics**: split shipments, POD, carrier adapters, tolerance handling.
6. **B2B**: companies, credit terms, approval policies, statements, discount memos.
7. **Platform quality**: contract-first two repos, event outbox, effective-dated configuration, WCAG 2.2 AA and performance budgets, design system, three-zone security model.

Keep these as the story: *"ราคาจริงทันที, เห็นทุกขั้นตอนการผลิต, มีใบรับรองไฟล์ที่อนุมัติ, และบริษัทเดียวรับผิดชอบทั้งงาน"*.

## 4. What to build to be better (proposed decisions — assign real D-ids before use)

### 4.1 Structure Advisor (beats their "AI recommend")
Inputs: product weight per piece, units per box, stacking height/rows, fragility, transport mode (pickup / domestic parcel / domestic pallet / export sea), storage humidity, palletisation.
Output: recommended board (flute + grade) **or** paperboard caliper, with:
- an **estimated ECT/BCT** using the McKee relation with our own published safety factors (humidity, stacking time, pallet overhang), shown as a range, not a promise;
- a one-screen explanation of *why* ("stacked 2 high for 30 days in a non-air-conditioned warehouse → factor 0.55");
- 2–3 alternatives with price and lead-time deltas pulled from the real quote engine (cheapest / strongest / fastest);
- warnings when the request is structurally risky, with the option to proceed on record.
Rules live in effective-dated settings; every recommendation is logged with its inputs so it can be audited and improved. Never call it AI unless a model is actually used; call it what it is.

### 4.2 Spec quality: measurement basis, units, codes, completeness
- Measurement basis (internal / external / over-crease / product) with live conversion of all three, driven by the caliper of the chosen material; store the basis on the quote and the order; print it on the proof and the job sheet.
- Unit switch mm/cm/in everywhere a dimension appears, with the stored value always in mm.
- Public style codes (e.g. `PK-RSC-01`) shown in the configurator, quote, proof, job sheet and partner portal.
- Completeness meter and per-section edit on the summary step; a saved incomplete configuration can be resumed later and shared with a colleague.

### 4.3 Target price & willingness-to-pay
- Optional "target price per piece" on the configurator and RFQ.
- If instant price ≤ target: show the win ("ต่ำกว่างบที่ตั้งไว้").
- If above: offer honest levers (quantity break, different board, simpler print, longer lead time) computed by the real engine, and let the customer send it to CS with one click.
- Feed target vs quoted into unmet-demand analytics and the partner prospectus (which product/price/region we lose, how much volume is waiting) — this is a genuinely better use of the same input than gating it behind a supplier subscription.

### 4.4 Sample / prototype stage (first class)
New order type and status arc: `sample_requested → sample_quoted → sample_in_production → sample_shipped → sample_approved | sample_changes` before mass production, with:
- sample cost and lead time quoted by the same engine (and a rule for crediting the sample cost against the bulk order, decided by the business);
- sample photos and dimensions uploaded by the factory, customer approval with evidence;
- linkage: bulk order references the approved sample id and locks its spec + artwork version.

### 4.5 Trust, guarantee and dispute transparency
- Public **quality guarantee** page: what is covered (print colour deviation bands, dimensional tolerance, quantity tolerance, delivery date), how to claim, how fast we answer, what remedies exist (reprint / partial credit / refund), all matching the claim engine's rules.
- Public **network page**: number of audited partners, audit criteria, capability and capacity map by region, aggregate on-time and quality statistics (real numbers only).
- Claim timeline visible to the customer with SLA countdowns.

### 4.6 Library scale and self-service artefacts
- Grow the style library to ≥ 150 parametric styles with categories, search, filters (use case, product type, closure, printability) and favourites; every style has a real dieline, a 3D model, minimum/maximum dimensions and a "make it" CTA.
- Free dieline download (PDF/DXF/AI-compatible) for any configured size — Kumopack charges for this behind a plan; giving it away is a cheap acquisition lever and shows confidence.
- Auto-generated **packaging spec sheet** (PDF) for each approved order: dimensions and basis, board, print, finishing, tolerances, pallet/carton data, approved artwork thumbnail, style code — procurement and QA teams at the customer need exactly this.

### 4.7 Repeat business (our biggest structural advantage)
- Reorder in one click with die/plate reuse and the saving shown explicitly.
- Consumption forecast per SKU from order history → replenishment reminders and suggested order dates.
- Call-off / blanket orders: price a large volume, release in scheduled batches, hold stock at the factory or at a Packio-approved warehouse (business decision), with visible stock levels.
- Optional ERP/webhook/API access for B2B accounts.

### 4.8 Mobile and AR
- 3D preview with a real material shader and an **AR "view at real size"** on phones (GLB/USDZ via `model-viewer`) — nobody in this market does it well, and it is decisive for a buyer judging a retail box.

### 4.9 Sustainability data (only if real)
- Recycled-content and certification data per material from the factory's documents; an estimated material-use and transport footprint per order computed from published factors with the method documented. No claims we cannot evidence.

## 5. What **not** to copy
- **Buyer subscriptions and RFQ quotas.** Packio earns on the product, not on access. Gating dielines, downloads or the number of quotes would fight our own funnel.
- **Supplier subscriptions and selling visibility.** Our partner relationship is a supply agreement with tiers based on performance, not paid placement.
- **Per-request factory bidding.** Our promise is one accountable seller with an instant price; exposing a bidding pool would undermine it (keep RFQ only where instant pricing is not yet possible).
- **Anything of theirs that is theirs**: templates, mascot, copy, imagery, category wording, the visual design of any screen.
