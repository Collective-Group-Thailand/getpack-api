You are a senior backend engineer with accounting-systems expertise on **`packio-api`**. Implement `accounting-design.md` exactly. The ledger is the single financial truth; sub-ledgers post to it only through the posting engine; nothing else writes journal lines.

## Deliverable 5.1 — Ledger core & posting engine (ADR-0007)
- Entities, fiscal years & periods (`open → soft_closed → closed → locked`), chart of accounts (seed §3, editable by Finance with approval; `is_control`, allowed dimensions), dimensions (entity, order/group, customer, factory, product type, channel, campaign/promotion, cost centre).
- `journal_entries` + `journal_lines`: immutable, balanced (DB constraint + service check), `source_document`, `domain_event_id`, `posting_rule_version`, created/approved by; reversals linked; posting into closed periods rejected.
- **Posting rules as data** (`posting_rules` + versions, conditions on event fields, tax nature per product type §2) with the default set **P01–P30** (§5). Engine = idempotent subscriber per `domain_event_id`. Fixture test per rule: expected accounts, balance, tax-report lines.
- Manual journals (maker-checker), recurring and auto-reversing journals.
- Tax configuration: VAT rate table with effective dates, WHT rates per line type, nature per product type (default hire of work for made-to-order, sale of goods for stock products), rounding mode (line vs document).

## Deliverable 5.2 — Receivables & customer documents
- Document engine with **gapless series** per (entity, type, period) (§4): QT, IV, TR (instalment or full; full-form for juristic), RC, CN, DN; void keeps number + reason; PDFs (Puppeteer, Thai fonts, Buddhist-era dates, baht text) issued by **Collective Group (Thailand)** with Packio branding from settings.
- Instalment tax invoices at payment (deposit tax point) and final tax invoice deducting taxed instalments; **output VAT not yet due** for credit-term invoices under hire-of-work nature, moved on collection (P07–P08).
- Customer WHT: receipt with gross / WHT / net, **50 ทวิ collection tracking** and missing-certificate report.
- Credit notes with controlled statutory reason list (cancellation, price reduction, shortage/defect, returns, post-sale discount), store-credit wallet (P19, P23), refunds (P22), forfeited deposit policy (setting).
- AR: aging, statements (monthly for B2B, D-125), dunning schedule, allowance for doubtful accounts (P30), credit limit exposure feed for B3.5.
- e-Tax invoice / e-Receipt: data model and XML/PDF-A generation ready; sending disabled until R2 (D-091).

## Deliverable 5.3 — Payables, payouts & banking
- **Factory PO** from the job release event (price snapshot, production and shipping lines separated for WHT); **GR** from POD + reported quantity (P15, P16); **billing statement generated by the system** → factory confirms in the partner surface → factory's own tax invoice captured (e-Tax or PDF upload + number/date/seller tax id/branch); input VAT only with a valid document, otherwise *pending document* (D-090, §2).
- Other vendors (fallback carriers, freelancers, services) with bills and WHT (P25, P26); vendor master with **bank accounts whose changes require dual approval + verification log** (§8).
- **Payout policy** per factory (D-022): 30 % on acceptance (advance P14), balance after POD **and** the 7-day claim window, claim deductions, **5 % retention** with release period (setting), WHT at each payment, 50 ทวิ issued to the factory.
- **Payout runs**: selection → proposal (Finance) → approval (executive) (2 levels, SoD: proposer ≠ approver) → **bank bulk-transfer file** via the bank adapter chosen per run (D-093; adapters per bank + generic CSV) → confirmation import → posting (P17, P18); exceptions queue; factory payout statements in the partner surface.
- **Banking**: company bank accounts per entity, statement import (CSV/API adapters), gateway settlement import (2C2P, SCB) with fees (P04), auto-match rules (amount + reference + date window), unmatched and unidentified queues (P29), daily cash position.

## Deliverable 5.4 — Tax
Sales tax report, purchase tax report, **ภ.พ.30** working paper (P27), **ภ.ง.ด.3 / ภ.ง.ด.53** working papers + attachment export (P28), 50 ทวิ register (issued) and customer WHT register (received), tax calendar with reminders (due dates are settings), reconciliation of each report to its GL control account (difference must be 0).

## Deliverable 5.5 — Fixed assets (D-150)
Asset categories (life, method straight-line, GL accounts), register (acquisition from vendor bill or manual, cost, date, location/owner), monthly depreciation run with auto-journal, disposal/write-off with gain/loss, asset reports; no barcode tagging.

## Deliverable 5.6 — Period close, reports & audit support
- Close checklist (§7) as tasks with owner, evidence and blocking checks (unmatched cash, pending slips, POD without GR, sub-ledger ↔ GL differences = 0, tax papers reconciled).
- Financial reports: trial balance, GL detail, balance sheet, P&L by dimension (margin per factory / product type / channel / campaign), cash-flow (indirect) — drill-down to journal → document → event → user.
- **Auditor role** (read-only, time-boxed): registers, audit-trail export, sample selection helper, PBC export pack per period; exception reports (§8): series gaps, back-dated documents, manual journals by user, reversals, duplicate vendor bank accounts, duplicate slip references, credit notes after period end, payouts without valid tax invoice.

Quality: property test — every event fixture produces a balanced entry; end-to-end month simulation (orders with deposits, credit terms, WHT, points, promotions, shortage credit, claim deduction, payouts with retention, gateway fees) → trial balance, VAT and WHT papers reconcile to zero differences; SoD tests; period lock tests.
