# P4 — company sales desk, discount approval with waterfall, playbook admin · plan (autorun)

Branch `feat/agent-p4-sales-desk` (both repos). Decisions D-469 (sales desk: inbound pool, assignment, SLA,
dashboard) and D-470 (discount approval with waterfall, field-level margin visibility, price lock, negotiation
history, sales playbook admin, credit cost estimate, post-deal review) — PLAN A-07, A-10, A-12, A-13, §7. The owner
delegated every decision and is not watching: assumptions are recorded here, in `docs/PROGRESS.md` and in the
decision rows; the most conservative option is taken for money, tax and factory confidentiality.

## Principles kept
- **Factory confidentiality** (D-435, D-463, D-044): no factory name, count, cost, buy price or lock reaches the
  store / agent zone. Agents and reps never receive cost or margin fields — the serializers that would carry them
  are not reachable for those permissions (absence tests, not hiding).
- **No profit wording**: the waterfall line is "กำไรหลังหักค่าคอม" (contribution after commission), never
  "กำไรสุทธิ"; agents see no margin at all (D-464).
- Money = numeric(14,2) + decimal.js + decimal strings; times timestamptz, business rules in Asia/Bangkok.
- Every state change: one transaction + audit / activity + outbox event; notifications are subscribers.
- Settings effective-dated and audited with an owner role; values no decision gives are defined `unset`.

## Scope (API)

### 1 · Staff pipeline (A-07)
Employee agents (`SalesAgent.kind = employee`, `staffUserId`) work their leads from the admin zone with their staff
account over the **same** `sales_agent_leads` model and services as the agent zone (no fork).
- Permissions: `sales.pipeline.self` (new role **`sales_rep`**: own employee agent only) and `sales.pipeline.manage`
  (sales_manager, c_level, vp, director; Superadmin by "everything"). Acting on a lead that is not one's own is
  audited (`sales_lead.acted_for`).
- Endpoints (`/v1/admin/sales/…`, tag `admin.salesLeads`, `admin.salesQuotes`, `admin.salesReps`): list (cursor,
  same filters as the agent zone + `agentId`, `agentKind`), get (ETag), create (rep: own agent; manager: `agentId`
  of an employee agent), update (If-Match), changeStage, addActivity, listActivities; quotes on behalf
  (`admin.salesQuotes.create` / `list`), reps list for pickers. Freelance agents' leads are read-only for managers.
- `docs/rbac.md` regenerated by `pnpm rbac:build`.

### 2–4 · Inbound pool, assignment, SLA
`sales_inbound_leads` (PDPA: purpose = answer a sales enquiry; retention = 2 years after closing or until the
contact asks; access = Sales): `source` (`contact_form` | `rfq` | `line_chat` | `manual`), source reference
(ticket no. / RFQ code), contact name / phone / e-mail, organisation, need, `status` (`new` | `assigned` |
`contacted` | `converted_to_lead` | `disqualified` + `disqualifiedReason` enum + note), `assigneeUserId`,
`firstResponseDueAt` (= created + `sales.first_response_hours` working hours: the `calendar.business_hours` window
(09:00–18:00) **every day** as P-05 says "ทุกวัน 09:00–18:00"), `firstRespondedAt`, `slaBreachedAt`, `agentLeadId`.
Append-only `sales_inbound_assignments` (from → to, mode, reason, actor).
- Sources: (a) `support.ticket_opened` with `queue = sales` (D-458 routing of topics quote / business) — contact data
  read from the ticket in the subscriber, never from the event (PDPA); (b) `rfq.submitted` by a customer with no
  placed order group ("non-customer") who is not linked to an agent; (c) LINE OA chats tagged sales — **deferred**:
  conversations have no tags yet (recorded); (d) staff manual create.
- Assignment (`sales.assignment_mode` manual | round_robin, seed `manual`): round robin picks the active
  `sales_rep` (with an active employee agent) with the fewest open items below `sales.max_open_per_rep` (unset = no
  cap), ties by least recently assigned. Reassign needs a reason (audited). Assigning registers a
  `SalesAgentLead` for the rep's employee agent through the agent programme's own `registerLead` (protection,
  neutral duplicate check). A contact that cannot be a lead (existing customer, another agent's protected lead or
  deal) keeps the assignment with `leadRefusal = unavailable` — one neutral reason (D-440).
- SLA job `sales.inbound_sla` (every 5 min): a breach sets `slaBreachedAt` once and records
  `sales_inbound_lead.sla_breached` → sales managers (in-app, e-mail, web push).

### 5 · Manager dashboard
`admin.sales.dashboard` (`sales.pipeline.manage`): funnel by stage (counts + drop-off), win rate, median days per
stage (from the timeline's stage changes), by source, by rep, by period (week / month, Bangkok), inbound
first-response SLA %, freelance vs employee split. `admin.sales.exportDashboard` → CSV (formula-safe).

### 6 · Discount approval desk + waterfall + price lock (A-12)
`sales_discount_approvals` — one per discount request round (P3 `sales_agent_discount_requests`):
- Opens automatically when a request is made (`sales_agent_discount.requested` subscriber) and on demand
  (`admin.discountApprovals.open` for a request opened before P4). **Prerequisite**: every quoted quantity has a
  buy price (factory price list / platform rate card / factory RFQ answer; `quote_options.buy_cost_net`) — else
  409 `discount_approval.cost_unknown` and nothing opens.
- Level (pure, unit-tested): `rep` when pct ≤ `sales.discount_authority.repMaxPct` (3.00) and margin ≥ floor;
  `sales_manager` when ≤ `managerMaxPct` (8.00) and margin ≥ floor; else `c_level`. Optional amount caps
  (`repMaxAmount`, `managerMaxAmount`) are defined but unset. `negotiation_heavy` customers move one level up
  (c_level stays). SLA `sales.discount_approval_hours` (4 working hours, same working-hours rule); the job
  `sales.discount_approval_sla` escalates an overdue approval one level once (`…escalated`), notifying the next
  level. Deciders: rep level — any `sales_rep` or higher; manager level — `sales_manager` or C-Level; C-Level level — `c_level` (Superadmin any). The requester never decides; a Superadmin may self-approve
  only under solo-owner mode with a typed reason (D-449), flagged and audited.
- **Decide** (`Idempotency-Key`): `approve` with 1–3 options (the first = full-rate `standard`, mandatory — the
  P3 `offerOptions` rules, called in the same transaction), `reject` with reason, or `counter` with give-get
  conditions (request closed `rejected` with the counter on it; the agent may open a new round with changed terms).
- Snapshot: SHA-256 of lines (option ids, quantities), costs (buy, shipping paid), terms (pct, give-get, payment
  template, credit days). Any difference at decision or apply time voids it (`stale`, 409
  `discount_approval.stale`). A re-quote after the quote's validity (D-436) keeps the approval only when lines,
  quantities and terms match and the margin at today's cost is still ≥ floor.
- **Price lock**: when the commission base ≥ `agent.deal_registration_threshold`, approval records a
  `sales_factory_price_locks` row per quantity (factory, buy price, price-list version) valid until the approval's
  validity. No partner-zone confirmation flow exists → status `requested`, partner UI **deferred** (when it comes:
  validity = the shorter of the two).
- **Waterfall** (server-computed, decimal strings) per quote option and per offered option: list revenue ex VAT,
  discount, net revenue, factory cost, shipping difference (charged − paid), payment fee by method
  (`payments.method_fees`, unset → 0 flagged), samples / free services, gross margin (amount, %), agent commission
  (rate, amount), credit cost estimate (item 9), bad-debt provision (`finance.bad_debt_provision_pct` by credit
  grade, unset → flagged), contribution after commission ("กำไรหลังหักค่าคอม", amount, %), floor check
  (`margin.floor_pct` by product class = product category code; default copied from D-012's 10 %).
- **Field-level RBAC** — three serializers chosen server-side: `authority` (sales_rep; and the agent never sees the
  desk) = `authorityLevel` green | amber | red only; `margin_pct` (sales_manager) = percentages and floor check only;
  `full` (c_level, vp, director, finance_manager, superadmin; `discount_approvals.waterfall_full`). Every view /
  simulation is written to `sales_waterfall_views` (append-only) and listed by `admin.salesWaterfallViews.list`
  (Superadmin, Auditor). Export (CSV) only `discount_approvals.export` (Finance Manager, Superadmin).
- **Simulator** `admin.discountApprovals.simulate`: discount %, credit days, payment schedule template, co-fund /
  spiff rate, payment method, free services → recomputed waterfall in the caller's tier; nothing saved.
- **P3 deferrals**: an accepted option makes a **new discounted quotation** (clone of the quoted quantity with
  `discount_amount`, valid `quotes.discounted_validity_days` = 7 days, the old one superseded, the agent's link
  moved to it) plus a discount memo of scope `sales_quote` that checkout applies to orders from that quotation
  (the existing `discounts` extension; margin-floor cap unless C-Level approved below floor); the quote PDF prints
  the conditions line (`sales_discounted_quotes`).

### 7 · Negotiation history
`admin.salesNegotiations.company` (per customer or company): rounds per quote, average approved discount, win /
loss, lost reasons, last competitor named; `negotiation_heavy` when rounds ≥ 2 on ≥ 2 of the last 5 quotes
(`sales.negotiation_heavy` setting). `admin.salesNegotiations.lostToPrice`: lost-to-price by product class and
quantity band (decade bands < 1k, 1k–10k, 10k–100k, ≥ 100k — a reporting convention, not a business value).

### 8 · Playbook admin
`SalesPlaybookCard` gains `owner_role` (marketing | sales_manager), `submitted_by/at`, `published_by`,
`change_note`. Admin CRUD `admin.salesPlaybook.*` (list, get, create draft, update draft, submit, publish (a
different person, `sales_playbook.publish`), retire, versions). P3 seeds stay; the seed ensures every PLAYBOOK.md
card exists idempotently (11 cards). Store read unchanged.

### 9 · Credit cost estimate (management figure, not posted)
Pure function: factory cash-out legs (from the factory payout policy: advance % at acceptance = day 0, balance at
POD + claim window; or the request's terms) funded FIFO by customer receipts (payment schedule template / deposit /
company credit term D-462 counted from delivery); each leg costs `amount × finance.cost_of_capital_annual_pct ×
days ÷ 365`, **rounded half-up to the whole baht per leg**, then summed. Golden tests: PLAN §7 → ฿6,642 + ฿4,757
= ฿11,399 = 1.06 % of ฿1,080,000; 50 % deposit + balance on delivery → ฿0.

### 10 · Post-deal review
`admin.salesPostDeal.list` (`sales_reports.read`): per won deal with an approval — approved vs actual gross margin
and contribution (actual invoiced quantity incl. tolerance, actual shipping cost, refunds), grouped by approver,
agent and customer; tiered like the waterfall (manager: % only).

## Risks
- Quotes are write-once: the discounted price is a new quotation; checkout honours it through a memo of scope
  `sales_quote` — orders from the old quotation never get it.
- No partner confirmation flow for price locks: the lock is a recorded request only.
- Round robin without a capacity setting spreads evenly with no cap until Sales sets one.
- LINE OA chat tagging does not exist: source (c) deferred.
