0. Working agreement (owner ↔ Claude)

  • Claude is the head of the getpack business team. It answers business-team questions and decides on the owner's behalf. Anything legal, tax or binding on real money goes to the named signer, and ships as config that stays off until signed.
  • Every round proposes improvements and new features for every page. The owner accepts them all by default. Each one ships behind its own flag.
  • Design must look like a skilled human designed it, never AI-generated. That means:
    • the existing getpack tokens and type only;
    • ledger-style lists instead of floating cards;
    • real wording instead of explanatory filler;
    • few callouts, and only where the customer must act.
  • Every round delivers:
    • an audit of every route at 1440 and 390 px (read-only in UAT);
    • a canvas prototype (HTML, published as an Artifact) with a "ดูสิ่งที่เปลี่ยน" panel;
    • a prompt pack with 🟦 API and 🟩 Web boxes per phase;
    • this kind of .md record;
    • a zip.
  • Test accounts on UAT (localhost:3001): phone 08000001xx and OTP 000000. C01 มาลี, C02 วิชัย, C03 นภา, C05 สุดา, C06 อนันต์, C07 ปรีชา (juristic, WHT), C08 ปริญญา, C09–C11 company UAT-CO-001, C12 ชัยวัฒน์, C13 อรุณี.

4. Decisions added in this audit

#Decision
P-24A reorder price is a quote. It locks for 15 days like any saved quote, not until noon the same day.
P-25Claims are decided within 5 working days of the claim date. At 3 working days with no decision, the CS lead gets an alert. The customer sees one status and the due date.
P-26Shown lead time comes from data per product. When it is longer than the 5-working-day standard, the page says why (material or product type). The business team reviews the 10-day value on corrugated postal boxes.
P-27Non-agents opening any /account/agent/* page see the join page, never an error.
F28New flag FEATURE_COMPANY_WEBHOOKS_UI: manage webhook subscriptions and delivery history in /account/company/api.
F29New flag FEATURE_APPROVAL_BUDGET (from the Stitch round): optional branch budget; approval shows used / after-approval; over budget flagged, not blocked.
F30New flag FEATURE_DOCS_RECONCILE (Stitch round): per-order table of non-voided tax invoices against the order total.
F31New flag FEATURE_STUDIO_BRAND_LOCK (Stitch round): "ใช้เฉพาะสีแบรนด์" switch and edge-distance check in the Studio.
Brand kitCustomer fonts allowed (P-22). Brand kits are free for every account. Quotas: individual 20 fonts, company 200 fonts, 2 GB per account. The customer attests the licence on upload; takedown within 1 working day of a rights notice. Locks and the pre-order check are for company accounts, default "warn". Font and logo files of closed accounts are deleted after 90 days, except files tied to ordered jobs (2 years). Print text is outlined via HarfBuzz shaping (Thai-correct). Production never receives font files. Sign-off S-06 (legal): licence attestation text and takedown process.
